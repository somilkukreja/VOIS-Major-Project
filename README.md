Analysis Performed:

Compared production across seasons.
Compared revenue and profit across seasons.
Compared total agricultural costs.

Key Insight to Display:
Seasonal conditions have a measurable impact on agricultural productivity and economic performance.

Crop-wise profit comparison

Key Insight:
Crop productivity differs across crops and is associated with variations in soil nutrients and farming inputs.
